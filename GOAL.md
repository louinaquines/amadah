# AmaDah Pastries — Goal.md

## Why this page exists

AmaDah Pastries currently sells through Facebook posts/stories and DMs
only. There's no place to send a new customer that shows the full current
lineup, feels trustworthy, and ends in an actual order. This landing page
is that place.

## Primary goal

**Convert a visitor into a DM or call within one scroll.** Every section
either builds appetite/trust or points at the order action — nothing is
on the page "just because a bakery site usually has it."

Success looks like: someone taps a Facebook post link, lands here, sees
what's available this week, and taps "Message us on Facebook" or dials
the number — on mobile, without hunting for contact info.

## Secondary goals

1. **Show the current lineup clearly.** Cinnamon Roll, Korean Garlic
   Cream Cheese Bread, the Cookie Club (Midnight / Golden / Classic),
   Crinkles, S'mores Cookie — with the newest drop (e.g. Biscoff Crunch)
   visibly flagged as new, matching how AmaDah already announces flavors.
2. **Establish trust fast.** A short, real "our story" note (home-based,
   started 2026, made with love) does more here than a long brand essay —
   it's a home baker, not a franchise, and the copy should sound like one.
3. **Make ordering frictionless.** Phone number, Facebook page, and
   general location (Balamban Cebu / Mandaue City) visible without
   scrolling to the very bottom — plus a persistent order bar on mobile.
4. **Surface social proof.** AmaDah doesn't have collected reviews yet, so
   the testimonials section ships with a small set of placeholder quotes
   written to sound like real customer comments (short, specific to a
   flavor, casual tone) rather than generic marketing lines. Swap these
   for real ones as soon as AmaDah has them — see roadmap.md.
5. **Be easy for AmaDah to update.** New flavors get added often (Biscoff
   Crunch, Crinkles, S'mores all appeared recently) — the menu section
   should be simple enough that adding one more card doesn't require
   touching layout code.

## Non-goals (explicitly out of scope for v1)

- No online ordering, cart, or payment — DM/call is the entire funnel.
- No account system, backend, or database — static content only.
- No blog, recipe pages, or multi-page site — this is a single landing
  page (see roadmap.md for the one-page structure).
- No delivery-tracking or inventory features.

## How we'll know it's working

Since there's no backend, "working" is judged qualitatively for v1:
- A first-time visitor can name what's on the menu and how to order
  within a few seconds of landing, without instructions.
- The page doesn't feel like a generic template — it should be
  recognizably *AmaDah*, using the brand's own colors, wordmark, and
  the torn-paper photo style already present in their product posts.
- It works cleanly on a mid-range Android phone on mobile data, since
  that's how the real audience will find it (Facebook → tap link).
